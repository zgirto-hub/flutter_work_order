How a work order flows
1
Issue is reported
Any staff member spots an issue and creates a work order from the app — describing the problem, selecting the target department, and optionally attaching photos or files.
2
Technician is notified & assigned
Technicians in the relevant department receive an instant push notification. An admin or the system assigns the right technician to take ownership.
3
Work is done — updates posted
The technician updates the work order status, adds comments, and uploads evidence. The reporter and any watchers are notified of every change in real time.
4
Work order is closed & logged
Once complete, the technician closes the WO. The closure time and responsible person are recorded permanently — ready to appear in the next report.

Notification controls
In-app notifications
A notification bell shows your unread count, updated every 20 seconds. A sound plays when new activity arrives while you have the app open.
Push notifications
Get alerted on your phone even when the app is closed. Sent the moment a comment or status change happens on a WO you're watching.
Watch any work order
Follow any work order as a watcher to stay updated, even if you're not assigned to it. Remove yourself at any time.
Personal preferences
Mute all notifications, or toggle specific types independently — comment alerts, status updates, push, and in-app — per your preference.
